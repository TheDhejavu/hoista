---
title: "What is Hoista?"
description: "A control plane for the projects you run on your own hosting accounts."
---

Hoista is a control plane for the projects you run on your own hosting accounts: Cloudflare, Vercel, Railway, Fly.io, Neon and more. It doesn't host anything. Your services and data stay at their providers, and Hoista gives you, your CLI and your AI agents one place to check status, read logs and bills, deploy and roll back. Your provider credentials stay in your own credential store.

Hoista is for developers who run projects across several providers and want one view of them. It also suits anyone who wants a coding agent to work on those projects without giving it their API tokens.
