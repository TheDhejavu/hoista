---
title: "Claude"
description: "Connect Claude on the web, desktop or mobile to your Hoista."
---

Claude runs in the cloud, so it needs Hoista at an HTTPS address it can reach. Either:

- **Hoista on your computer, through a tunnel or proxy** pointed at port 8788. See [From your computer](/agents/tools/chatgpt#from-your-computer) on the ChatGPT page; the steps are the same.
- **Hoista on a server:** a [remote](/guides/remote) or a [self-hosted Hoista](/guides/self-host). **Agents → Connect an agent** in the desktop app shows its address.

Your Hoista URL is that address followed by `/mcp`, for example `https://hoista.example.com/mcp`.

Custom connectors work on Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. Once added, the connector works in Claude on the web, the desktop app and mobile.

## Add Hoista

**On Free, Pro and Max:**

1. Go to **Customize → Connectors** and choose **Add custom connector**.
2. Name it **Hoista** and paste your Hoista URL.
3. Choose **Add**.

**On Team and Enterprise**, an owner adds it once in **Organization settings → Connectors → Add → Custom**. Each member then goes to **Customize → Connectors**, finds Hoista and chooses **Connect**.

## Approve it

When Claude connects, Hoista asks you to allow it. On a server, its sign-in page opens: sign in with your passkey. On your computer, a page opens there instead, and Hoista asks for Touch ID or your password. Choose what Claude may do and which projects it can reach, then choose **Authorize**.

Claude connects over HTTP, where Hoista can't ask you questions in the chat, so Hoista offers it only **View only** or **Full access**. **Ask for approval** needs the tool to ask you. Start with **View only**, and widen it later in [Agent permissions](/agents/permissions).

## Use it in a chat

In a chat, open the **+** menu, then **Connectors**, and make sure Hoista is on.

## Check it works

Ask Claude: "What projects do I have on Hoista?" It should list your projects. If it doesn't, see [If it doesn't connect](#if-it-doesnt-connect).

Using Claude Code on your computer? It connects differently: see [Claude Code](/agents/tools/claude-code).

## If it doesn't connect

- **Claude can't reach the URL:** open it in a browser. You should see your Hoista's sign-in page.
- **To remove it:** in **Customize → Connectors**, open Hoista's menu and choose **Remove**, then revoke the agent in **Agents** or with `hoist agents revoke`.
