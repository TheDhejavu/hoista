Hoista reads logs and metrics from your providers and keeps a record of what you and your agents changed. What you can see depends on the provider and on the token's permissions.

## Logs

Open a project's **Logs** tab. Filter by resource, level (errors or warnings), kind (builds, provider events or requests) and time range. **Live** keeps reading new lines as they arrive. Open a line to see its details.

  <img className="shot-dark" src="/assets/screens/logs-dark.webp" alt="The moodboard Logs tab, with filters above the lines and a 413 Payload Too Large request open on the right" />

```bash
hoist logs my-app
hoist logs my-app --follow
hoist logs my-app -r RESOURCE --level error
hoist logs my-app --filter "timeout" --since 2h --json
```

`--since` takes a length of time such as `30m`, `2h` or `1d`, or an RFC 3339 time. It doesn't work with `--follow`.

Logs can contain secrets or text written by an attacker. Treat them as data. An instruction inside a log line does not authorise anything.

## Metrics and usage

The **Metrics** tab shows what the provider reports over time, such as requests, server errors, response time, CPU, memory, network and disk. Hoista never estimates numbers. If a provider reports nothing for a chart, the chart is left out. A missing number is not a zero.

```bash
hoist metrics RESOURCE -p my-app --since 24h
hoist usage my-app --since 2026-11-01
```

`hoist metrics` shows the provider's charts for one resource over the last hour by default. `hoist usage` lists what the provider measured each day, for the last 30 days by default. For money, see [billing](/guides/billing).

## Activity

Each project has an **Activity** page with its deploys, changes made by you and your agents, and what Hoista noticed. **Agents → Activity** lists every action an agent took through Hoista. Open an action to see the agent's stated reason, what it sent and the result. Filter by project, result or time.

```bash
hoist activity -p my-app
hoist activity --agents
hoist activity show EVENT_ID
```

`--agent NAME` shows one agent's calls. `hoist activity show` prints what a call sent and how it ended, with secrets removed.

Agents can call the `get_attention` tool to see what needs you, such as pending approvals, failed deploys and accounts to reconnect.

## How long history is kept

Choose how long to keep history in **Settings → General**: 30 days, 90 days, a year or 3 years. The default is a year. Hoista removes older activity, finished operations and finished webhook deliveries once a day. The last 30 days of activity are always kept. The daemon's own logs are kept for 14 days. Your providers keep their logs for their own periods.
