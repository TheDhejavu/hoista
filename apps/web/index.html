Connect the AI
you already use.

Hoista speaks MCP, the standard AI tools use to connect to apps. Set it up once and your AI can see, deploy and fix your apps.

In a coding tool

Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Windsurf. Run this once in your terminal:

$ claude mcp add hoista -- hoist mcp

In a chat app

Claude, Grok, Gemini or ChatGPT. Open Hoista, copy your private link, and paste it into the app's connector settings. Only you can use it.

Questions

Do I have to move my apps?

No. Import them from Railway, Vercel or Cloudflare and they stay where they are. Hoista only reads until you choose to manage.

Which AI tools work?

Claude, Grok, Gemini and ChatGPT, plus Cursor, Claude Code and Codex. Anything that speaks MCP. In ChatGPT, deploying needs developer mode, which not every plan has.

Does anything go through you?

No. Hoista runs on your computer and talks to your hosts directly. There's no Hoista server and no account. Chat apps reach it through a private link on your own Cloudflare account.

Which accounts do I need?

Usually just Cloudflare to start. Hoista tells you when an app needs another one and walks you through it.

Can I switch providers later?

Yes for apps and jobs. Hoista redeploys them on another connected provider and shows the cost first. Databases stay where they are.

What does it cost?

Hoista is free. You pay your hosts directly, and most experiments cost nothing while idle. Some hosts don't report exact costs per app, so Hoista estimates those from their published prices.
