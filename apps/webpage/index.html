Connect the AI
you already use.

Hoista speaks MCP, the standard way AI tools connect to apps. Set it up once and your agent can see, deploy and fix your apps.

In a coding tool

Run this once in your terminal. Works even when Hoista’s window is closed.

$ claude mcp add --scope user hoista -- hoist mcp

In a chat app or cloud agent

Point a tunnel at port 8788 on your computer, or set up a remote, then paste your Hoista URL into the app’s connector settings. You allow each one first.

https://hoista.yourdomain.com/mcp

Questions

Do I have to move my apps?

No. Connect Railway, Vercel or Cloudflare and your apps stay where they are. Hoista only reads until you choose to manage one.

Which AI tools work?

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor on your computer. ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and cloud agents like Hermes through your remote. Anything that speaks MCP.

Does anything go through you?

No. Hoista runs on your computer and talks to your hosts directly. There’s no Hoista account and no Hoista server. Your remote runs on hosting you’ve connected, in your own account.

Can an agent do something I didn’t want?

Risky actions wait for your approval, and each agent has its own limits on what it can do and which apps it can reach. Every action is recorded.

Which accounts do I need?

Usually just one host to start. Hoista tells you when an app needs another account and walks you through it.

What does it cost?

Hoista is free. You pay your hosts directly for what they run, and Hoista shows what each one has billed you this period, side by side. A remote runs on one of your hosts, like any of your apps.
